Privacy Policy for Flag Quiz: Atlas Compass

Publisher: Get.ng Technologies Limited
Contact: m@get.ng
Effective date: September 25, 2026

Flag Quiz: Atlas Compass (shown as Atlas Compass on your device) is a geography game for iOS and Android. The game can be played without an account. Its questions, flags, maps, and atlas entries are included in the app.

Information kept on your device

The app keeps your quiz attempts, mistakes, mastery, scores, and preferences, including your language and sound choice, on your device. We do not operate an account or game-progress server and do not receive that game-progress information through the app. You can remove it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app, subject to any device backup or restore service you use.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to show banner ads and occasional interstitial ads between completed rounds when ads are available. Every ad request is configured for child age treatment and G-rated ad content. We do not request interest-based ads or remarketing for any player. The Android app does not request permission to access the Android advertising ID. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may still collect and share an IP address, interactions with the app or ads, diagnostic information, and other device identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. An IP address may indicate an approximate location. We do not request your precise device location, contacts, or photos for this game.

See Google's information about data used by sites and apps, Google's AdMob privacy information, and Google's Android and iOS ad SDK disclosures for more detail.

The app configures Google's User Messaging Platform to treat every player as under the age of consent for ad privacy messaging, so it does not request personalized-ad consent. If a privacy-options form is available in your region, you can reopen it from Settings → Privacy options in the app. You can also change advertising or tracking settings on your device. The game remains playable offline and when an ad is unavailable.

Sharing and retention

We do not ask you to provide a name, email address, or contacts to play. We do not sell your locally stored game progress. Google and its enabled advertising partners process advertising information under their own policies and retention periods. Local game progress remains until you remove the app or clear its data, subject to device backups.

Children

The game is designed for teens and adults. It does not ask players for their age. Because younger players may still open it, all ad requests receive child age treatment and G-rated ad content. If you have a question about a child's privacy or data associated with this app, contact us at m@get.ng.

Questions and changes

Contact m@get.ng with privacy questions or requests. We may update this policy if the app's features or advertising partners change. The effective date above identifies this version.
